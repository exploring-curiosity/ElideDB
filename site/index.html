what it is

A database, not a folder of files

Every table is Parquet under a transaction log. Every video is indexed to the byte at ingest. Every query is planned to touch the minimum, and what it touched is counted and reported.

Timestamp native

Video frames, GPS, IMU, audio: one canonical timeline. Ask for any window and get aligned frames and sensor rows back, decoded on demand through byte-range reads.

Time travel included

Appends, compaction, deletes: each is one atomic commit. Any earlier version of a table stays readable until you vacuum it away.

Event search, no labels

Describe the event in plain words. World models and video-native encoders score every clip, fused with weights learned from your data. Nobody annotates anything.

principles

Rules the engine is built on

Raw data is immutable. Ingest never re-encodes your source or writes into it. The store carries its own transcoded media and serves even after the original is gone.
No dataset priors in code. Nothing in the engine knows it is looking at kitchens, roads, or factories. Whatever matters in your corpus is learned from your corpus.
No language model judges. Retrieval is scored by measurement: encoder evidence, learned weights, and pixel-level geometric verification.
Honest abstention. A query the data cannot answer returns nothing, with the reason, instead of ten confident look-alikes.
Every byte is counted. Each query reports bytes touched against bytes stored. In the cloud that number is your bill.
